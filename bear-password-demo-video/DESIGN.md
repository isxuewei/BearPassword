## Style Prompt

BearPassword demo video uses a light, trustworthy product-workspace style derived from the actual app screenshots: soft off-white canvas, crisp dark text, violet brand accents, and small mint/amber secondary highlights. The feeling should be calm, practical, and security-focused, like a polished software walkthrough rather than a launch trailer.

## Colors

- Canvas: #f5f3fb
- Surface: #ffffff
- Text: #171827
- Muted text: #606579
- Brand violet: #7c6cf6
- Mint support: #bff2e5
- Amber support: #ffe9b3

## Typography

- Headlines: "Songti SC", "STSong", serif
- Captions and UI labels: "PingFang SC", "Hiragino Sans GB", sans-serif
- Data and counters: "JetBrains Mono", monospace

## Motion

- Product screenshots enter with restrained slide, focus, and scale moves.
- Scene changes use blur crossfades as the primary transition and occasional horizontal pushes for topic resets.
- Captions enter cleanly after the visual context is visible so the viewer reads without racing.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use dark cyberpunk, neon, or purple-blue gradient styling.
- Do not hide the real UI behind heavy blur or decorative overlays.
- Do not use loud kinetic typography that competes with the product screenshots.
- Do not add voiceover or background music for this version.
